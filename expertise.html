Lifecycle Phase 01 & 02

Engineering & Construction Management

We deliver structural, civil, geotechnical, and MEP engineering solutions that push boundaries of resilience, seismic damping, and sustainable design. Paired with our industry-leading construction management practice, we handle some of the most complex building and infrastructure programs in the world under Construction Management-at-Risk (CMAR), Design-Build, and IPD delivery models.

  • High-tolerance geotechnical engineering and structural seismic modeling
  • Digital delivery and BIM 4D construction sequencing
  • Zero-accident safety culture and rigorous quality assurance
Lifecycle Phase 03 & 04

EPC Delivery & Program Management

Our program management services go beyond project management. We act as a trusted partner, assisting clients from the earliest planning, shaping, and strategy through execution and milestone delivery. Through comprehensive EPC delivery models, we take on single-point accountability to compress project schedules, optimize capital allocation, and achieve transformational outcomes.

  • End-to-end program governance and capital risk management
  • Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) and single-point contractual certainty
  • Earned Value Management (EVM) and real-time digital dashboards
Lifecycle Phase 05 & 06

Strategic Advisory & Architecture & Design

Our advisory practice helps clients navigate complex financial, regulatory, environmental, and operational hurdles throughout their asset lifecycle. Our architects and interior designers create inspiring, sustainable buildings and environments that uplift people, enhance operational efficiency, and establish enduring architectural identity.

  • Public-Private Partnership (P3) structuring and capital advisory
  • Bioclimatic architecture, natural light optimization, and luxury interior design
  • LEED, WELL, and IGBC Platinum green building certifications
Lifecycle Phase 07 & 08

Transportation & Civil Infrastructure + Electrical Networks

We plan, design, and manage the transit, highways, bridges, and smart city utility corridors that move people and goods efficiently while reducing emissions. Complementing this, our electrical infrastructure division executes high-tension substation grids, smart transmission lines, and automated telemetry.

  • High-capacity expressways, flyovers, and multimodal urban transit systems
  • 66kV to 400kV Gas Insulated Substations (GIS) and switchyards
  • Resilient underground utility corridors and smart city power grids
Lifecycle Phase 09 & 10

New Energy (Solar & Wind) + Turnkey Projects

Accelerating the energy transition. We deliver utility-scale solar photovoltaic parks, wind farms, and hybrid battery storage (BESS) solutions to power communities sustainably. With our comprehensive turnkey delivery model, we guide developers from land feasibility and engineering through construction, grid interconnection, and long-term asset operation.
